1. What SwipeThisCard is

SwipeThisCard is an educational tool that helps you keep track of the interest-free window each card in your wallet gives you on a purchase, based on the dates and facts you type in yourself. It does not connect to a bank, does not know your real balance, and does not place any purchase, transfer, or payment on your behalf.

2. Not financial advice

Nothing in the app or on this site is financial, credit, or investment advice, and none of it recommends that you spend, borrow, or open any particular card or account. Figures shown are calculations from what you typed in, not a prediction, an offer, or a guarantee. Your card issuer’s own statement is always the final authority on what you owe and when.

3. Accuracy is on you

The app calculates from the dates and facts you enter. If your issuer changes a due date, a rate, or a fee, the app has no way to find out on its own — there is no bank connection to check against. Keeping what you’ve entered current is your responsibility; the app will say so wherever it matters.
